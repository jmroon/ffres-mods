ANTI-ALIASING OPTIONS

Choose one variant:
- Menu (Anti-Aliasing-Options ZIP): adds Off / FXAA / TAA / TSR to Graphics
  Settings > AA Method and maintains your saved choice.
- TSR-only (TSR-Always-On ZIP): keeps TSR enabled, without changing the menu
  or accessing saved options.

Both maintain the AA method through loads and cutscenes. Other graphics
settings stay unchanged. TSR costs more GPU time than FXAA.

INSTALL

1. Close the game. Use only one variant; uninstall the other before switching.
2. For a game without UE4SS, use a Standalone ZIP: the loader is included and
   configured. Otherwise use the package without the loader to preserve your
   existing UE4SS configuration.
3. Extract the ZIP beside FFRS.exe, merging folders, then launch normally.
   Menu variant: choose Graphics Settings > AA Method and Apply.
   TSR-only: TSR is applied automatically once a player/world is available.

No Engine.ini edits are needed.

MANUAL UE4SS SETUP (only if using a package without a loader)

Tested UE4SS: v3.0.1-1181-g83330635 (non-dev).
https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/download/experimental/UE4SS_v3.0.1-1181-g83330635.zip
Place dwmapi.dll and the ue4ss folder in FFRS/Binaries/Win64, preserving
existing mods. Our tested setup uses these values in the existing
[EngineVersionOverride] section of ue4ss/UE4SS-settings.ini:
MajorVersion = 5
MinorVersion = 6

UNINSTALL / SWITCH VARIANTS

Close the game. Under FFRS/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods, remove the installed
variant's folder: AntiAliasingOptions for Menu, or TSRAlwaysOn for TSR-only.
For Menu, also remove zz_AntiAliasingOptions_P.pak/.ucas/.utoc from
FFRS/Content/Paks/~mods. Preserve other mods and your saves.
If you installed Standalone and no other mods use UE4SS, you may also remove
its dwmapi.dll and ue4ss folder from FFRS/Binaries/Win64.
