Anti-Aliasing Options

Two variants, one mod:
- Menu: choose Off, FXAA, TAA or TSR in Graphics Settings > AA Method. Your
  saved choice stays active through loads and cutscenes. The default is FXAA.
- TSR-only: automatically keep TSR enabled, without menu changes or save access.

Install only one variant. Each is available with UE4SS bundled (Standalone)
or without a loader for existing UE4SS installations. Follow README.txt in the ZIP.

Only the AA method is controlled. Rendering resolution, TSR history quality,
depth of field, bloom, exposure and the original AA quality setting stay unchanged.
TSR can reduce shimmer, especially in motion, at a higher GPU cost than FXAA.
No DLSS, DLAA, frame generation or resolution presets are included.

Tested: Windows Steam build 24250053 and UE4SS v3.0.1-1181-g83330635 (non-dev).
May conflict with other mods that force an AA method; the Menu variant also
replaces the graphics-menu table. Game updates may require a mod update.
