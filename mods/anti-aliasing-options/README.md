# Anti-Aliasing Options

Choose **Off / FXAA / TAA / TSR** in Graphics Settings → AA Method.
Your choice persists through loads and cutscenes. Other graphics settings
stay as they are. TSR offers better stability in motion at a higher GPU cost.

Requires UE4SS. [Installation and removal](INSTALL.txt).
