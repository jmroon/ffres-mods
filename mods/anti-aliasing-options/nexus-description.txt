Anti-Aliasing Options

Choose Off, FXAA, TAA or TSR directly from Graphics Settings. This mod adds
an AA Method row and keeps your saved choice active through loads and cutscenes.

The original Anti-Aliasing quality row stays available. The mod controls only
the AA method: rendering resolution, TSR history quality, depth of field,
bloom, exposure and other graphics settings remain controlled by the game.

TSR can reduce distracting shimmer, especially in motion, but costs more GPU
time than FXAA and can have temporal artifacts. No DLSS, DLAA, frame generation
or resolution presets are included.

Requirements
Windows Steam version; tested game build 24250053.
UE4SS v3.0.1-1181-g83330635, installed separately. See the included README for
the download source, engine-version override and complete installation steps.

Usage
Open Graphics Settings, select AA Method, choose your option and Apply.
The default is FXAA. The game saves your choice in its native option data.

Installation / removal
Follow README.txt in the download. Merge the supplied FFRS folder into the
game folder after installing UE4SS. No Engine.ini changes are needed.
To remove, close the game and delete this mod's three menu containers and
AntiAliasingOptions mod folder. Keep other mods and shared UE4SS files.

Compatibility
May conflict with other mods that replace the graphics-menu table or force
an AA method. Future game updates may require a mod update.
