ANTI-ALIASING OPTIONS

Adds Off / FXAA / TAA / TSR to Graphics Settings > AA Method and keeps your
choice active through loads and cutscenes. TSR costs more GPU time than FXAA.

INSTALL

1. Close the game. Install UE4SS: place dwmapi.dll and its ue4ss folder in
   FFRS/Binaries/Win64. Preserve any existing UE4SS mods and settings.
   Tested: v3.0.1-1181-g83330635 (non-dev).
   https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/tag/experimental-latest

2. In ue4ss/UE4SS-settings.ini, update the existing section:

   [EngineVersionOverride]
   MajorVersion = 5
   MinorVersion = 6

3. Extract this mod's FFRS folder beside FFRS.exe, merging folders.
4. Launch the game, choose Graphics Settings > AA Method, then Apply.

No Engine.ini edits needed. Other graphics settings stay unchanged.

UNINSTALL

Close the game. Remove zz_AntiAliasingOptions_P.pak/.ucas/.utoc from
FFRS/Content/Paks/~mods and the AntiAliasingOptions folder from
FFRS/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods.
