TSR ALWAYS ON

Enables TSR automatically and keeps it active through loads and cutscenes.
No new menu option, save edits, or Engine.ini changes. Rendering resolution,
TSR history quality, DOF, bloom and exposure remain controlled by the game.
TSR costs more GPU time than the original FXAA.

INSTALL

Close the game. Choose ONE package and extract its contents beside FFRS.exe:

- Standalone: for a game without UE4SS. Includes the loader, configured with
  the Unreal 5.6 override used in our tested setup. No manual setup needed.
- Script-only: for an existing compatible UE4SS installation. Adds only this
  mod and leaves your loader configuration alone.

Use TSR Always On OR Anti-Aliasing Options, not both. To switch from the menu
version, remove ue4ss/Mods/AntiAliasingOptions and the three
zz_AntiAliasingOptions_P files (.pak/.ucas/.utoc) from FFRS/Content/Paks/~mods.
UE4SS and its Mods folder are under FFRS/Binaries/Win64.

Launch normally. TSR is applied once a player/world is available. The game's
existing AA quality option remains; this mod maintains TSR as the AA method.

UNINSTALL

Close the game and remove FFRS/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/TSRAlwaysOn.
If you installed the Standalone package and no other mods use UE4SS, you may
also remove its dwmapi.dll and ue4ss folder from FFRS/Binaries/Win64.
